Battle for Sunshine

Sunshine, Victoria · a WW2 “what if” · real-time strategy

How to play

Goal: destroy the enemy army, or inflict heavy casualties until its morale breaks and it surrenders. Lose 70% of your own army, or let your morale collapse, and you surrender.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Left-click a unit to select it; drag a box to select any mix of units as one group (Shift adds)
  • Right-click ground: move in formation · right-click an enemy: attack · right-click your truck with infantry selected: board it
  • F then click: attack-move · H hold · X stop · U unload truck
  • Ctrl or Shift + 1–9: save group · 1–9: recall (press twice to jump to it)
  • Camera: WASD/arrows or screen edge to pan, Q/E or right-drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, middle-drag to pan
  • P/Esc pause · Space centre on selection

Touch

  • Tap a unit to select it; tap ground to move, tap an enemy to attack
  • Box-select: turn on ▢ Select and drag, or long-press then drag
  • One finger drags the map; pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate
  • Group bar: tap Save then a number to store the selection; tap a number to recall it

Units

  • Infantry: cheap; much harder to hit inside buildings (they stand on the roof); grenades hurt tanks up close
  • Tanks: strong, thick front armour, weak sides and rear; buildings block them
  • Artillery: very long range, but only fires at targets a friendly unit can see (spotters)
  • Trucks: fast; carry two infantry squads and resupply ammo and morale to units near them

Morale drops with casualties and nearby losses and recovers when units are left alone. Broken units retreat to base until they rally.
